T.R.'s Elkhorn Ranch, 1886

The ranch house and outbuildings rebuilt at full scale from T.R.'s own 1886 photographs, Bill Sewall's letters, the 1957–59 National Park Service excavations and the Library's 360° survey of the foundation stones. The rooms are furnished from T.R.'s and Sewall's descriptions and the objects Taylor dug up in 1959. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan. What the model is built from

Positions: house footprint fitted to the surviving foundation stones in the Library's 360° panoramas (long axis 11.7° east of true north, ±3°); outbuildings placed from the Beaubien–Taylor 1957/59 site plan registered to that footprint (±4 ft for nearby features, more toward the corral). Absolute GPS placement is ±12 m until surveyed on site with NPS. Model origin: centre of the house footprint, 47.24191, −103.62226.
