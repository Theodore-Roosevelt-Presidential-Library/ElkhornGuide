    Two quick permissions

    The guide works like a map app. It needs your phone's location and compass to show where you are and point you to each stop.

    1. Location

    Your phone will ask, "Allow elkhornguide.labs.trlibrary.com to use your location?" Tap Allow. On iPhone, leave Precise turned on, or the guide can't tell when you reach a stop.

    2. Compass

    iPhones ask separately for "motion and orientation". That is the compass. Tap Allow so the arrow can turn with you.

    Your location is used on your phone to place you on the map. The Library does not receive or keep it. You can turn it off any time in your browser settings.
